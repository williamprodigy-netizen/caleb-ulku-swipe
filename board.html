A free-live-class funnel for local SEO agencies. Not our market — swiped for its mechanics. The one worth stealing is the survey gate: the thank-you page tells you your registration is not complete until you answer five questions, bribes you with a cheat sheet for doing it, and drops a $49 upsell on the other side. That is a show-rate device, a segmentation engine and a self-liquidator in one screen.

Opts in → told the registration is NOT complete

“Congratulations on registering and securing your spot … but do not close this page yet because your registration isn't quite complete … it's only once you've submitted the survey that your spot will be fully confirmed.” Then: “as a thank you for filling it out, I'm going to send you my 14-day local SEO ranking cheat sheet.” Show rate is our keystone metric and this is a working answer to it — a second commitment, a segmentation payload and a lead-magnet delivery reason to email, all bought with one screen.

Completes the survey → $49 VIP page

The anchor is not a fake retail price, it is a real recurring one: “to get this kind of access to me normally, you'd have to be inside my pro community, and that runs $197 a month. But today you're not paying $197. You're not even paying $100. Right here on this page you can get the full VIP experience for just $49.” A three-step descent from a price the prospect can verify. Scarcity is capped at the first 100 and tied to a real constraint — he can only go deep on so many websites live.

VERIFIED · OTO video transcribed 6 Aug (05_vip_oto_pitch.md)
Buys VIP → 7-day trial of his own software

The $49 does not just buy attention, it buys a product trial: “I'll give you full access for seven days to use it on a real client. If it doesn't deliver, cancel, no drama.” Product-led growth bolted onto a webinar funnel. The buyer is inside the tool before the class has even run, which makes the class a product demo rather than a pitch.

VERIFIED · OTO video transcribed 6 Aug
Phone is required for a free class

Five required fields including a mobile number and a combined SMS + email consent box. That is real friction on a cold Meta click, and he is paying it deliberately — the number is what makes an SMS reminder layer possible. Whether the trade is worth it is exactly the test we should be running on our own opt-in, in both directions.

The scarcity furniture is dead on a live cold page

The sticky header reads “234 FREE Spots Left. Closing in 00D : 00H : 00M : 00S” and does not move. The number is hardcoded and never decrements; the countdown is frozen at zero. The event date “August 6 @ 1:00 PM ET” is hardcoded eight times in the markup, and the cold ad was still serving at 3pm ET on 6 August — after the class had already ended. Somebody is paying for clicks onto a dead timer.

One body copy, nine hooks

All ~9 live ads carry identical body copy and an identical headline; only the 27–56 second video varies. The body has run unchanged since at least 17 June across 160 total ads. They are not testing copy — copy is settled. They are testing hooks, and they re-upload the winner rather than rewriting it. The argument itself is one sentence: AI can't fix a sink, so local service search is the one category AI cannot absorb.
